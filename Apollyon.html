Apollyon

General Overview

Apollyon was the very first game I made with a team for the final game project of my junior year at Questar III Gaming and Multimedia. It follows Ivan, a miner, working for a russian mining company using the latest in mining technology. Upon the discovery of an ancient deity deep underground, the company sends Ivan, unknowingly, in a heavy industrial exoskeleton into the deep mines to end the threat.




My role

For this project I took the role of programmer and became the manager gradually through development. For programming I had built everything from the ground up using the construct 3 engine. Besides programming the mechanics and the like, my big roles were to implement art and audio into the game which proved to be easier than expected. As mentioned earlier I took on the role of manager about a quarter into the project. This job was alot more challenging than programming because on top of actually building the game I had to make sure my team got me the assets I needed within deadlines. However we prevailed, and the final game product turned out to be pretty good for a group of teenagers with minimal game development experience.
