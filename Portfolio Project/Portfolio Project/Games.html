Apollyon

Apollyon was the first game I made with a team and was also the final project for my junior year of Questar III gaming and Multimedia. It follows Ivan, an employee for a russian mining company, unknowingly going on a suicide mission to kill a newly awakened eldrtich god who is threatening earth. I did the coding and level design for everything in the game in construct 3. Some of the features I am most proud of are the drone mechanic that alows you to deploy a tiny rover to squeeze into small areas and the chase sequence.




Bready Or Knot: Fully-Baked

Bready or Knot: Fully-Baked is a recreation of another game that originated as my first ever game project named Bready or Knot: Half-Baked that I made during my junior year of High School. Since the first concept of Bready or Knot it was a class meme. For specificly Bready or Knot: Half-Baked I made the entire game with no help. For Bready or Knot Fully-Baked however I am the main programmer and director and I had a team of 5 other individuals.
