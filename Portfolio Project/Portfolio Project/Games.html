We made this game as the final project of our gaming and multimedia class for our junior year of high school. The inspiration for this game came was origininally a cave diving horror game but it evolved into something much more different. As it evolved it went from just a simple "Your in a cave with bad things happening" to your being forced to try to kill an eldritch god who started to attack the secluded mining site Chernoye Zoloto out of spite for being experimented on. My part in this game was level assembly and programming. Some of the harder things for this game was coding the drone as trying to cycle between 2 playable characters in construct 3 is kind of a hassle.
