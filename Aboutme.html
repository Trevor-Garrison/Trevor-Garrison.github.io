About Me

Hey, how's is going. My name is Trevor Garrison. I kinda sorta have loved videogames since being a small child and like many others have though of concepts and ideas for games both new and old. With this being said I have persued becoming a game developer with the Questar III Gaming and Multimedia Program. During which all of the things currently on this portfolio are from. My most proud project is Bready Or Knot. This was originally concepted and prototyped during the first year of Questart III but then fully realized during the second year for New York Skills USA of which it then went to compete against many other schools. Although it didn't win any medals, it did win the hearts of many, many people who stopped by and those I was competing against. But this is the about me page, not about Bready or Knot page. Other than my creations some funny factoids about me are I am a pretty good cook, I have a potentially unhealth amount of time poured into Helldivers 2 and Dead By Daylight, and I am a triplet and identical twin.
